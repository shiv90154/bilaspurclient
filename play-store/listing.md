## Full description (4000)

```
DHĪ Ayurveda Classroom is the official app of Dr. Pardeuman Singh's coaching for AIAPGET (PG entrance) and AMO (Ayurvedic Medical Officer) exams.

Dr. Pardeuman Singh (BAMS, MS Shalya Tantra) has prepared Ayurveda students for more than 15 years, and about 400 of them have been selected in AIAPGET and AMO exams. He serves as Associate Professor in Shalya Tantra, so every topic is taught with the exam and the patient in mind.

WHAT YOU GET IN THE APP

• Live classes: join your batch's scheduled classes. Attendance is marked automatically, and you get a reminder before each class.
• Study notes: clear PDF notes sorted by subject and topic, opened right inside the app.
• Tests and test series: timed exam-style tests with negative marking, instant results, your rank in the batch and a full answer review.
• Doubt solving: ask a doubt with a photo of the question. Your teacher replies in the app and you get a notification.
• Video lessons: recorded lessons to revise a topic at your own pace.
• All courses: see every course of the institute with its full syllabus.
• Free demo: register and try the demo notes and tests before your admission is approved.

SAFE AND PRIVATE

• No ads, and your data is never sold.
• Study material is protected, and one account works on one phone at a time.
• Delete your account any time from Profile → Delete my account.

WHO IT IS FOR

BAMS students and graduates preparing for AIAPGET, state AMO and central AMO exams.

The app is for students of DHĪ Ayurveda Classroom. Your batches, classes and notes open once the institute approves your admission. To join a course, visit dhiayurved.com or contact the institute.

Questions? Email or WhatsApp us; the details are on dhiayurved.com/contact.
```

> The description must not mention prices, offers or "buy" in the app (Play payments policy).
> "Visit dhiayurved.com or contact the institute" is fine because it names no price and has no link that sells.
> Keep it that way when the client asks for edits.
