## Target audience and content

- Target age group: **18 and over** only.
  Reason: the courses are AIAPGET and AMO preparation, which are for BAMS students and graduates. Choosing 18+ keeps the app out of the Families policy (extra rules for apps aimed at children).
- "Could the app unintentionally appeal to children?" **No**.
- If the client says school students also use it, choose **13–15 / 16–17 / 18+** instead and tell us: the app already asks a guardian to agree for under-18 users, but the Families rules then also apply.

## Payments (not a form, a rule)

The app must never show a price, an offer, a "Buy" button, or a link or WhatsApp button that leads to buying a course. Course sales happen only on dhiayurved.com. This is Google's payments policy; breaking it gets the app removed.
